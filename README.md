# RHS Music & Arts Attendance

A centralized attendance management platform for the music and arts programs at Redmond High School.

🌐 **Website:** https://rhsattendance.org

## About

RHS Music & Arts Attendance is a web-based platform designed to simplify attendance tracking for Redmond High School's music and arts programs.

The platform provides a streamlined way for students to check in to events and enables authorized staff to manage attendance records. Its goal is to make attendance tracking more efficient, organized, and accessible for the school community.

## Features

- **QR Code Check-In** — Convenient digital check-in for school events.
- **Manual Check-In** — An alternative check-in option when scanning a QR code isn't possible.
- **Event Attendance Tracking** — Organized digital attendance records.
- **Secure Account Access** — Accounts and permissions for authorized users.
- **Staff Management Tools** — Attendance workflows designed for program staff.

## Purpose

This project was created to improve attendance management for school music and arts activities by replacing cumbersome manual workflows with a more streamlined digital experience.
